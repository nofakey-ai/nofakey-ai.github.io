About us

We believe everyone deserves a quick, honest check

Nofakey.ai was built because fake news and scams are a real problem, and most tools to fight them are complicated or paywalled.

Pattern-based checking — not a claim-by-claim fact database.
Nothing you paste is stored on our servers.
Free to use — no account or payment required.

What we're actually offering

What we offer is a fast, free first-pass check — a way to catch the most obvious scam and misinformation patterns before you share something. It's a starting point, not a verdict, and we always encourage checking important claims with a trusted news source.

We built Nofakey.ai for the moment right before you hit "forward" — when a message feels urgent, exciting, or alarming, and a five-second gut check can save you from spreading something false or falling for a scam.

Honest about what the tool can and can't do
Never sell or store your submitted text
Free for everyone, no hidden fees
Our approach

Why we keep it simple

A pattern check is fast and useful, but it isn't the whole picture. We'd rather be upfront about that than oversell it.

Fast, not exhaustive

We check wording patterns in seconds. We don't verify every fact in a message — that still takes a human and a good source.

Private by default

Checks run without storing what you paste. There's no account, no history, and nothing tied back to you.

Pointing you onward

When something looks suspicious, we tell you where to verify it — trusted outlets, fact-checkers, and original sources.
